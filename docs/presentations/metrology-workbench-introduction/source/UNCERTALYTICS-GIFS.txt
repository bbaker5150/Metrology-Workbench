INSTRUMENTS AND MODEL
UUT: Fluke 87V digital multimeter, normal 6000-count mode. DC accuracy +/- (0.05% of reading + 1 count). 6 V range has 0.001 V resolution; 60 V range has 0.01 V resolution. The example uses the 6 V range at 2 V and 5 V, then the 60 V range at 9 V.
Reference: Agilent 34401A 6.5-digit DMM (manufacturer documentation now hosted by Keysight), fixed 10 V DC range. One-year accuracy at 23 +/- 5 deg C: +/- (0.0035% of reading + 0.0005% of range). Range resolution 0.00001 V. This is a manufacturer specification, not a fabricated calibration-certificate uncertainty.

Illustrative reference-comparison method: both meters observe a stabilized DC source. The reference specification is modeled as a rectangular bound. UUT resolution contributes one step divided by sqrt(12). Assumed residual source instability is +/-20 microvolts and thermal EMF is +/-2 microvolts, both rectangular. These two lab terms are illustrative assumptions, not manufacturer claims. Reference resolution/noise is covered by its stated accuracy and not added separately. Terms are assumed uncorrelated with effectively infinite degrees of freedom. This simplified demonstration model is not a completed laboratory uncertainty evaluation or certificate.

At 5 V, the 34401A accuracy bound is 225 microvolts. Combined standard uncertainty is approximately 316.77 microvolts. Expanded uncertainty changes from approximately 620.86 microvolts at 95% confidence (k=1.960) to 815.94 microvolts at 99% (k=2.576). Independent arithmetic checks for all points are in uncertalytics-numeric-checks.json. Risk and TUR values are the application's own outputs using its resolution-aware tolerance handling; they should not be inferred from unrounded instrument accuracy alone.

Risk assumptions: 85% assumed end-of-period reliability, 12-month interval, required TUR 4, required PFA 2%, required REOP 85%. These are demo targets, not a recommended laboratory policy. At 9 V the coarser UUT resolution reduces measurement capability, which is intentionally visible.
