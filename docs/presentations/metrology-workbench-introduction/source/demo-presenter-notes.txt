1. Metrology Workbench

Metrology Workbench brings three complementary tools into one home screen. Run Calibration handles supported Y5020 and A40B calibration workflows. Uncertainty Budget carries saved Type A results into a wider analysis of measurement uncertainty and decision risk. Report of Calibration imports saved calibration results into an editable report. Users review instrument definitions, analysis assumptions and report metadata as part of their work. The architecture supports additional modules and calibration capabilities in the future.

Sources: user-supplied Tasking.docx; Frontend/workbench/src/app/HomeLauncher.jsx; Frontend/workbench/src/app/moduleRegistry.jsx. Home layout follows the image in Tasking.docx. Calibration artwork is an AI illustration based on user-supplied Photo 1.jpg. Other visual elements are illustrative. The distribution is conceptual; the simplified sum of variances assumes uncorrelated contributions expressed in common output units.

4. Uncertainty Budget

All text, diagram boxes and arrows on this slide are native editable PowerPoint objects. The diagram is conceptual, not a numerical result or a plot of measured data. Contributions are expressed in common output units through the measurement model, including sensitivity coefficients and correlations where applicable. Expanded uncertainty applies a coverage factor to combined standard uncertainty. The module can import saved Type A results, use shared instrument definitions, assess test uncertainty ratio and decision risk, and explore guardband options.
